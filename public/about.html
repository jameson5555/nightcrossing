About Nightcrossing

Nightcrossing is a themed crossword game by Jameson MacArthur, built around a quiet nighttime atmosphere and puzzles you can return to at your own pace.

A collection of subjects to explore

Each puzzle brings together words from a subject such as food and cooking, music and sound, nature, or space. Instead of treating every clue as an isolated question, you can use both the theme and the letters from crossing words to find your next answer.

The game keeps track of unfinished and completed puzzles. As you finish collections, further themes become available, and journey badges mark your progress. You can move between puzzles without having to finish the current grid first.

Help when a word won't come

Correct words lock into the grid so that their crossing letters become reliable starting points. A free word hint in each puzzle gives you another way into an unfamiliar answer. Banked hints can unlock further guidance or reveal a letter, and completing puzzles replenishes your supply.

The playing guide explains the controls, the difference between word hints and letter reveals, and how to use crossings before reaching for extra help.

Play in your browser

The browser game works with a physical keyboard or a phone's on-screen keyboard. You do not need an account to start. Guest progress stays on your device; optional Google sign-in, where enabled, lets you save to an account and continue across devices.

Some versions may offer an optional advertisement for an extra hint. You can keep solving without watching it. Details about progress storage, optional accounts, analytics, and advertising are in the privacy notice.

Puzzle updates and feedback

Puzzle collections are assembled from themed word, clue, and hint pools using automated layout and quality checks. Those checks help catch structural and content problems, but they do not guarantee that every clue is clear. Feedback about a misleading clue or an awkward answer helps identify entries that need attention.

For a clue report, include the theme, puzzle title, clue number, and direction. For a technical problem, include your browser or device and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not send passwords or private account credentials.
